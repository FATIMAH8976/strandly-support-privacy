Common questions
How do I delete my data?
Open Strandly → Settings → Delete my account and data. This permanently erases your profile, scan history and routine from your phone and from the cloud backup, and removes the calendar events and reminders Strandly created. It happens immediately. It does not cancel a subscription — manage that in your Apple ID settings. If you can't open the app, email us and we'll delete your cloud backup for you.
How do I restore my data on a new phone?
On your old phone, open Settings → Save my data with Apple ID (do this once). On the new phone, tap "Sign in with Apple" under "Optional — restore data from a previous install" on the first screen and use the same Apple ID — your scan history and progress come back. If you never linked your Apple ID, the data can't be recovered on a new phone.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Open Strandly → Settings → Manage subscription, or go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Strandly. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Cancelling doesn't refund the current period; refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I restore a purchase after reinstalling?
Open the Unlock Pro screen (tap "Unlock Pro" on the Home tab) and tap "Restore Purchase" at the bottom. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
Why can't Strandly see my scalp in my photos?
The AI needs both photos to clearly show your scalp. Retake them in good light, with your hair parted so the scalp is visible inside the guide frame, and hold the phone steady. Photos of anything else can't be scored.
Who sees my scan photos?
Only after you give permission, your two photos are sent over an encrypted connection to OpenAI for analysis. Strandly doesn't save them in the cloud, and the temporary copies on your phone are deleted when you leave the scan. You can withdraw permission in Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Is Strandly a medical app?
No. Strandly gives general wellness information and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Please see a doctor or dermatologist about hair loss, pain, bleeding or any change in your scalp.
