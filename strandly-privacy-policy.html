Privacy Policy

Strandly is a scalp and hair health app. This page explains, plainly, what the app collects, what it's used for, and — since Strandly doesn't run its own servers — where your data actually lives.

01Information we collect

Strandly collects only what it needs to personalise your scalp and hair results. There's no account or sign-up — nothing here is tied to an email address or login.

Strandly does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind — there is nothing in the app collecting behavioural or usage data beyond what's listed above, and how we collect it is always the same: directly from what you type or photograph inside the app, never inferred or purchased from anywhere else.

02How we use it

  • To compute your scalp health score and generate personalised recommendations
  • To let the in-app Hair Coach chat answer questions using your quiz answers and latest scan
  • To build a hair-care routine and, if you choose to connect it, add reminders to your device calendar
  • To process subscription purchases and confirm what you're entitled to

03Where it's stored

No account · no server database

Strandly has no backend server and no database. Your onboarding info, quiz answers, and scan history are stored locally on your device only, using standard iOS on-device storage. Nothing is synced to the cloud, and nothing is visible to us unless you send it to us directly (for example, by emailing support).

This also means: if you delete the app, or use the in-app "Delete my account" option in Settings, that data is gone — including from any backup you don't separately control — and we have no copy to restore it from.

Each also processes data under their own privacy policy and retention rules, and we only send what's strictly needed for the feature you're using at that moment.

05Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone outside the third-party services listed above that are necessary to run the app. There is no other category of recipient — no affiliates, no "partners," no undisclosed parties.

06Your choices & consent

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete my account inside the app, or simply uninstall it — both permanently and immediately erase everything Strandly has stored on your device. There's no waiting period, no support ticket required, and nothing held back on a server, because none exists.

07Children's privacy

Strandly is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. Strandly is not listed in the Kids Category.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date and version number at the top of this page. Since Strandly has no accounts or push notifications tied to an identity, we can't email you directly about changes — checking back here is the way to stay current. This page is linked both from the App Store listing and from inside the app itself (Settings → Learn more), so it's always reachable the same way.
