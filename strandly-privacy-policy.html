Strandly collects only what it needs to personalise your results. You never create a username or password, and we never ask for your email address.

Strandly has no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. It does not collect your location or contacts, and it does not read your Photos library — scan photos are taken with the camera and are not saved to your Photos library. Your device calendar is used only if you choose to sync your routine, and those events are created on your device and are not uploaded.

02How we use it

  • To calculate your scalp score and generate personalised observations and recommendations
  • To build a hair-care routine and, if you choose, add it to your device calendar and send reminders
  • To back up your progress so you can restore it on a new phone
  • To process your subscription and confirm what you're entitled to
  • To keep the service working and prevent abuse

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build profiles to share with others, and we do not use it to train AI models.

03Where it's stored & how it's protected

On your phone + an encrypted cloud backup

On your phone. Your profile, answers, results and routine are kept in Strandly's private storage on your iPhone, which iOS protects when your phone is locked.

Cloud backup. A copy of that data — never your photos — is saved to a database hosted by Supabase and tied to your random anonymous ID. This is what lets you restore your history on a new phone with Sign in with Apple. Each person's rows can only be read or changed by their own signed-in session.

Encryption. Everything sent between the app and our service providers travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and our hosting provider encrypts stored data at rest.

Photos. Scan photos are sent over an encrypted connection to OpenAI for analysis and are not saved by Strandly. The temporary copies the camera makes on your phone are deleted when you leave the scan.

05Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for advertising. The only recipients are the service providers listed above, and only to run the features you use. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Open Strandly → Settings → Delete my account and data. This permanently erases your profile, scan history and routine from your phone and from the cloud backup, and removes the calendar events and reminders Strandly created. It takes effect immediately, with no waiting period.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription — manage or cancel that in your Apple ID settings. If you can't use the app, email us and we'll delete your cloud backup for you. Otherwise we keep your data until you delete it; OpenAI may retain photos it received for up to 30 days, as described above.

07Health information notice

Strandly provides general wellness information based on your photos and answers. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and is not a substitute for professional advice. See a doctor or dermatologist about hair loss, pain, bleeding or any change in your scalp. Product suggestions are general and are not endorsements — check the label, and ask a professional if you have allergies, a medical condition or take medication.

08Children's privacy

Strandly is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used Strandly, contact us and we will delete the data.
